# SupportIQ Cloud Plans and Included Limits

Limits reset at the start of each monthly billing cycle. Unused capacity does
not roll over.

## Starter

- Up to 5 agent seats.
- 1,000 AI-assisted conversations per month.
- 2 GB of knowledge-base storage.
- 1 active external integration.
- Email support with a two-business-day target response time.

## Growth

- Up to 50 agent seats.
- 10,000 AI-assisted conversations per month.
- 25 GB of knowledge-base storage.
- 5 active external integrations.
- Email support with a one-business-day target response time.
- Salesforce integration eligibility.

## Enterprise

Enterprise seat, conversation, storage, and integration limits are defined in
the customer's order form. Enterprise includes single sign-on, configurable
data retention, audit-log export, and priority support.

## Limit behavior

At 80 percent of a monthly conversation limit, SupportIQ emails organization
owners and billing administrators.

At 100 percent, Starter and Growth organizations can continue to view existing
conversations, but new AI-assisted answers are paused until the limit resets or
the plan is upgraded. Support agents can still send manual replies.

Enterprise overage behavior follows the customer's order form.
