# Resetting a SupportIQ Cloud Password

## Standard password reset

Users who sign in with an email address and password can request a reset from
the SupportIQ Cloud sign-in page:

1. Select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address associated with the account.
3. Open the most recent password-reset email.
4. Follow the reset link and choose a new password.

The reset link expires 30 minutes after it is issued. Requesting another reset
invalidates all earlier reset links.

The new password must contain at least 12 characters. It must include an
uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol. It cannot match
any of the user's previous five passwords.

## Single sign-on accounts

Users whose organization requires single sign-on do not have a SupportIQ Cloud
password. They must reset their password through their organization's identity
provider. SupportIQ support cannot create or reset an identity-provider
password.

## Missing reset email

The reset email normally arrives within five minutes. Before contacting
support, the user should:

- check spam or quarantine folders;
- confirm that the entered email address is correct;
- allow emails from `no-reply@supportiq.example`;
- wait five minutes before requesting another link.

Support agents must not reveal whether an unverified email address belongs to
an account.
