# Subscription Cancellation Policy

## Who can cancel

Only an organization owner or billing administrator can cancel a SupportIQ
Cloud subscription.

The authorized user can cancel from **Administration > Billing >
Subscription > Cancel subscription**. SupportIQ sends a cancellation
confirmation email to all organization owners.

## Effective date

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing cycle. The
organization retains access to paid features until that date and is not charged
for the next cycle.

SupportIQ does not provide a prorated refund for unused time after
cancellation. Refund eligibility is governed separately by the refund policy.

## Reversing a cancellation

An organization owner can reverse a scheduled cancellation before the current
billing cycle ends. After the cancellation becomes effective, the organization
must purchase a new subscription to restore paid features.

## Data after cancellation

When the paid period ends, the organization enters a 30-day read-only period.
Users can sign in and export data but cannot create new conversations or
ingest new documents.

After the read-only period, access is disabled and the data-deletion schedule
in the data-retention policy begins.
