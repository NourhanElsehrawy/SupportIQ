# Refund Policy

## First subscription purchase

A customer may request a full refund for the first purchase of a paid
SupportIQ Cloud subscription within 30 calendar days of the purchase date.
This guarantee applies once per organization.

The request must be submitted by an organization billing administrator through
the billing support form. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment
method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Renewals

Monthly and annual renewal payments are non-refundable. Customers can prevent
the next renewal by cancelling before the renewal date.

SupportIQ may refund a renewal only when:

- the same invoice was charged more than once;
- SupportIQ charged an amount different from the issued invoice; or
- applicable law requires a refund.

## Usage charges

Charges for usage above an included plan limit are non-refundable once the
usage has been processed.

## Plan changes

Upgrading a plan takes effect immediately, and the prorated upgrade charge is
non-refundable. Downgrades take effect at the next billing-cycle start and do
not create a refund for the current cycle.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically create a refund. Cancellation
timing is described in the subscription cancellation policy.
